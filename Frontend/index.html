Phishing Awareness

Learn how to spot phishing red flags, protect your personal data, and understand how cyber scams work. Knowledge is power!

🔎 Common Signs of Phishing:

  • Suspicious email addresses (e.g., support-paypal123@gmail.com)
  • Urgent language ("Your account will be suspended!")
  • Unfamiliar or mismatched URLs
  • Asking for sensitive information (passwords, OTPs)
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers

✅ Tips to Stay Safe:

  • Always double-check sender's email and URL.
  • Hover over links before clicking.
  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Keep your software and antivirus updated.
  • When in doubt, verify directly from official websites.

🚨 Example of Phishing vs Legit URL:

Phishing: http://paypal-login-security.com

Legit: https://www.paypal.com
